FAQ

Common questions.

How do plans and time work?

Every account is on a plan. Free by default. Your plan has a tier (Free, Plus, or Pro) and a time balance. A free 24-hour key adds one day to your plan time but keeps your tier at Free. A Plus or Pro key upgrades your tier and adds the key's days to your time. Think of it like a streaming subscription: you are always on a plan, the plan can run out, and redeeming a key extends it.

What is the difference between a free key and a paid plan key?

A free key gives you 24 hours of access on the Free tier. You can run Real, but some perks are not included. A Plus or Pro key does the same and also unlocks tier perks: skip the ad steps, shorter device-reset cooldown, priority support. Both types add time to the same clock. You can hold a Pro tier with years of time, or a Free tier with just a few hours.

What happens when I redeem another key?

Time always stacks. A new key extends your existing time from wherever it currently ends. Your tier only ever upgrades. Redeeming a Plus key while on Pro keeps you on Pro.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Keys activate a plan or add time to your Real account. Sign in before redeeming or using Real.

Where do I get plans?

Free keys come from projectreal.gg. Plus and Pro plan keys come only from our verified resellers. Avoid fake sellers, copied domains, and cracked builds.

What if I lose my key?

Once redeemed, your plan and time are tied to your account. The key itself is no longer needed. If you have not redeemed it yet, contact support within 7 days.

Can I use Real on multiple devices?

One device at a time. You can switch devices, but Real runs on one machine at once. Paid plans get a shorter device-reset cooldown.
